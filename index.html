Add to home screen

Open it like an app, nothing to download.

iPhone: Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Android: Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen.

Please note

Your location and tracks stay on this phone. Nothing is uploaded or collected, unless you tap Share location. This is in line with the principles of Malaysia’s PDPA and Indonesia’s UU PDP.

Do not lock the phone with the power button. The browser stops GPS when the screen locks. Tap Dim instead: the screen goes black to save battery, and tracking keeps running. Double-tap to wake.

GPS is a guide only. It can drift under thick canopy, and it needs your battery. Tell your group where you are going and bring a power bank.
